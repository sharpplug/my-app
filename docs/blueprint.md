# **App Name**: Moood

## Core Features:

- Aura Analysis: Analyze user's mood and skin condition based on photo or text input, providing personalized recommendations and dynamic theme change tool.
- Naya Wellness Coach: Provide personalized health and wellness advice tailored to the MEA region, including meal plans and product recommendations tool.
- Vibes Feed: Create and share ephemeral 24-hour content with interactions like likes, waves, and private messages.
- 360 Shopping Vibe: Enable business partners to upload 360-degree panoramic images of their store or products with interactive hotspots for direct shopping.
- SKIP Ride & Courier: Integrate ride-hailing and courier services within the app, including driver tracking and cancellation with reason feature.
- Partner Dashboard: Provide a centralized dashboard for business partners with AI content generation tools and AI Career Coach to enhance their business.
- Premium Digital Ticket: For events booked in-app, provide beautifully designed digital asset showing event details as well as the user's QR code for easy offline use at the event entrance.

## Style Guidelines:

- The overall vibe should be calm and restorative. Background color: Desaturated grayish blue (#D0DCE2). This color evokes tranquility, reflecting a peaceful and introspective mood.
- Primary color: Soft lavender (#B19CD9). This choice suggests serenity and healing, aligning with the app's goal of wellness.
- Accent color: Pale peach (#FFDAB9). This warm hue adds a gentle contrast, representing comfort and a touch of lightheartedness, preventing the interface from feeling cold.
- Body text: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, for clear, modern readability.
- Headline Font: 'Playfair', sans-serif, with thin-thick contrasts, providing an elegant, fashionable touch.
- Use minimalistic icons with rounded corners to maintain a soft, approachable feel.
- Incorporate smooth transitions and subtle animations to enhance user experience and provide visual feedback without being distracting.
